How Alpha and Beta work

Alpha inputs: net PPA, success rate, explosiveness, points per opportunity, havoc, turnover margin, neutral site, rest difference, and two opponent-rating features (offense and defense differences), blended with a four-game prior weight against 2025 team profiles. Beta is a full joint refit of Alpha's 10 features plus one signed tier_gap feature — not Alpha plus a fixed correction, so even tier_gap=0 games can show small differences between the two.

"Where the margin comes from" is the exact same linear math the model already uses — each feature's standardized value times its fitted coefficient — just broken apart and shown per feature instead of only as the final summed margin. Nothing is estimated or invented for that view.

Neither model sees market lines, totals, or moneylines as inputs. 2025 has already been examined repeatedly, so it is not a pristine holdout.
